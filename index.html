Knowzies

A 3D animated learning series for children aged 6 to 10. Six animal friends find out why it rains, why we sleep and how things float, The friends explain everything in clear, simple words. Every episode is dubbed and subtitled in many languages.

Watch Knowzies on YouTube

For parents and teachers

Every episode has one clear learning goal and is checked for accuracy before publication. The channel is set as made for kids on YouTube. Comments and personalised ads are off.

Knowzies Serienstudio

Knowzies Serienstudio is the internal production tool of 2Mountain-Development GmbH. It runs on our own computer and uses the YouTube API Services to upload our own episodes, captions, playlists and channel texts to our own YouTube channels Knowzies and ChronicleKNOW (documentaries for adults). It is not offered to the public. Details are in our privacy policy.
